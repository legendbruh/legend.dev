01 / About

A record of curiosity, still being written.

Legend is a student, a developer, and a builder who spends most of his time taking things apart or building stuff to understand how they work, then putting them back together differently.

The work moves across code, hardware, and half-finished machines too! Some of it ships. Most of it is the residue of asking what happens if, and following the answer wherever it leads.

There is no fixed discipline here, just an ongoing habit of learning by building, and an interest in the point where different fields start to overlap.
